# Night work policy — MétalPro

Synthetic document for the Flowpanel demo.

## Scope

This policy applies to every temporary worker assigned to a MétalPro plant, including the Valenciennes machining workshop.

## Night hours

Night work means any work performed between 22:00 and 05:00. A shift is a night shift when at least two hours fall inside this window.

## Night bonus

Hours worked at night are paid with a night bonus of 40 % of the hourly rate. The bonus is invoiced by the staffing supplier on a separate line of the invoice.

## Limits

A temporary worker may not work more than 7 hours on a night shift. Two consecutive night shifts must be separated by a rest period of at least 12 hours.

## Approval

Night shifts must be requested by the production manager at least 48 hours in advance and approved by the plant HR lead.
