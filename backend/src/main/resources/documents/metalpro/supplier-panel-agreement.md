# Supplier panel agreement — MétalPro

Synthetic document for the Flowpanel demo.

## Panel members

MétalPro's staffing panel has two suppliers: Proxi Staffing and Atlas RH.

## Response time

Suppliers must send their first candidate proposals within 48 hours of the order being published.

## Rates and markup

The supplier's markup coefficient is capped at 2.10 on the gross hourly rate.

## Invoicing

Suppliers send one invoice per month. Invoices are paid within 45 days.
