# Plant safety rules — MétalPro

Synthetic document for the Flowpanel demo.

## Safety induction

Every temporary worker attends a 90-minute safety induction on the first day, covering machine guarding and lockout procedures.

## Personal protective equipment

Safety shoes, safety glasses and hearing protection are mandatory in the machining workshop. Cut-resistant gloves are required at the press lines.

## Machine operation

Only trained operators may run CNC machines. Lockout-tagout is applied before any maintenance.

## Incidents

Any accident is reported to the plant HR lead within 2 hours.
