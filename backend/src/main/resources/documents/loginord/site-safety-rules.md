# Site safety rules — LogiNord warehouses

Synthetic document for the Flowpanel demo.

## Safety induction

Every temporary worker attends a 45-minute safety induction on the first morning, before any work on the floor. The induction is given by the site safety officer.

## Personal protective equipment

Safety shoes and a high-visibility vest are mandatory in all warehouse areas. Forklift operators also wear a hard hat in the loading bays. The staffing supplier provides the safety shoes; LogiNord provides the vests and hard hats.

## Forklift operation

Only workers holding a valid CACES R489 certificate for the category of the truck may drive it. Category 1 covers pallet trucks; category 3 covers counterbalanced forklifts up to 6 tonnes. A copy of the certificate must be attached to the assignment contract.

## Speed limits

The maximum speed for forklifts is 10 km/h inside the warehouse and 6 km/h in pedestrian zones.

## Incidents

Any accident or near miss is reported to the site safety officer within 24 hours. The staffing supplier is informed the same day.
