# Timesheet and overtime policy — LogiNord

Synthetic document for the Flowpanel demo.

## Submission

Staffing suppliers submit weekly timesheets every Monday before 12:00 for the previous week.

## Approval

The HR manager approves timesheets within 2 working days. Timesheets with anomalies are either approved as overtime or returned to the supplier for correction.

## Overtime rate

The first 8 overtime hours of a week are paid at 125 % of the hourly rate; further overtime hours are paid at 150 %.

## Maximum hours

No temporary worker may exceed 10 hours in a day or 48 hours in a week, even with overtime approved.
