# Supplier panel agreement — LogiNord

Synthetic document for the Flowpanel demo.

## Panel members

LogiNord's staffing panel has three suppliers: InterSud Intérim, Proxi Staffing and Atlas RH. Orders are published to all panel members at the same time.

## Response time

Suppliers must send their first candidate proposals within 24 hours of the order being published, and within 4 hours for urgent orders.

## Rates and markup

Workers are paid the same hourly rate as a permanent LogiNord employee in the same position. The supplier's markup coefficient is capped at 1.95 on the gross hourly rate.

## Invoicing

Suppliers send one invoice per mission, at the end of the mission, based on the timesheets approved in Flowpanel. Invoices are paid within 30 days. Any hour not approved in a timesheet is credited back with a credit note.

## Overtime

Overtime is only paid when it was agreed in the contract or approved by the HR manager on the timesheet.

## Termination

A supplier that fails the response time on more than 20 % of orders over a quarter can be removed from the panel with one month's notice.
