# Night work policy — LogiNord

Synthetic document for the Flowpanel demo.

## Scope

This policy applies to every temporary worker assigned to a LogiNord warehouse, including the Lille Lesquin and Roubaix sites.

## Night hours

Night work means any work performed between 21:00 and 06:00. A shift is a night shift when at least three hours fall inside this window.

## Night bonus

Hours worked at night are paid with a night bonus of 25 % of the hourly rate. The bonus is invoiced by the staffing supplier on a separate line of the invoice.

## Limits

A temporary worker may not work more than 8 hours on a night shift. Two consecutive night shifts must be separated by a rest period of at least 11 hours.

## Approval

Night shifts must be requested by the site manager at least 72 hours in advance and approved by the HR manager.
